Study Workstation

Sentinel Forge · Web

A calm, addressable place for what you're studying. Stored in this browser.

Board

A–Z columns × rows that grow as you add cards. Each card has a fixed address. Tap a cell to see or add cards — the board expands when you do.

Select a cell to work with its cards.

Library

Keep books and passages here. Paste text or upload a .txt / .md / .html file from this device.

Stubbed in v1 (named, not silent): .docx / .pdf parsing, OneDrive, Google Drive, Whisper voice dictation.

Retrieve

Keyword search across your library and cards. Ranked by how many of your words each item shares.

Off by default. When on, an answer is drawn only from your search hits and cited to them.

Type a word and search.

Assistant

A resident helper that reads your library and cards. Ask a question, or tell it to make a card or a note. It answers only from your own material and cites what it used.

Reading tint:
Uses Claude on your account; asks your consent on first use. It can create cards and notes for you — everything it does is logged.
Sentinel Forge Study Workstation · study-workstation-web · practice twin. Works alongside the Grok copy — material moves between them by JSON export, not a shared database. Retrieve = the retrieve stage of RAG (store → retrieve). No embeddings, no BM25. Design follows the BDA Dyslexia Style Guide (2023), the UK Home Office accessibility posters, and W3C COGA guidance.
